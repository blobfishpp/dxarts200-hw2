memory

Freshman year at UW.

My dad introduced me to my cousin, a PhD student also at UW. We live within a 10-minute walk of each other.

The same day we exchanged social media, she found out I love basset hounds. She happens to have one. We immediately went to have pizza for dinner. She brought Milky.

After that, we , I mean, me and my cousin, and me and Milky, became very close friends.

I walked her.
I took care of her when my cousin traveled.
I cooked for her. I bathed her.
I took her to Medina Park for picnics.
I bought a lot of things for her.
I made shirts with her face on it.

At some point,
“love Milky”
almost became a personality.

My cousin introduced me to her friends. Some became my friends too.

At the end of my freshman year, I had the offer to transfer to Tufts university in Boston. But I chose to stay at UW. It was a hard decision to make, because I consider my cousin and Milky to be important to me.
