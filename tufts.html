Boston

I accepted the transfer offer from Tufts.

Around the same time, my cousin told me that she and her husband, and of course, Milky, were planning to move to Boston after she graduated with her PhD.

For one year, I lived in Boston alone.

My cousin frequently sent me photos of Milky on my phone. Sleeping on her bed, going to the dog parks we've been to so many times.

Then eventually, I reunited with my cousin and Milky in person.

My cousin worked at a biotech company, and her husband worked at another biotech company.

Even though our schedules became busier, we still lived relatively close to each other near Tufts.

I still walked Milky. I still took care of her when my cousin traveled for work. She still occupies my heart.

Everything felt parallel to Seattle, just shifted into colder weather and a different school. Boston was cold, and occasionally, I miss Seattle.
