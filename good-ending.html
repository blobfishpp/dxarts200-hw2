somewhere better

The family who adopted Milky lived somewhere quiet and beautiful. Old-money people with enough time, enough space, enough love.

Milky chased squirrels every day in her new large backyard full of grass and trees.

Even if she no longer remembers Seattle, or my cousin, or me. She was loved, and will be loved to the very last millisecond of her life.

I wish her good health. I love her too.
