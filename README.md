# Following Milky

**DXARTS 200 — Assignment 2: Hypertext**

Visit the site: https://blobfishpp.github.io/dxarts200-hw2/

---

This project is inspired by my own personal identity. Using text, images, and sound, it imagines alternative futures for myself, especially, how my life might have unfolded differently depending on whether I stayed at UW or transferred to Tufts.

At the center of both paths is my relationship with my cousin and her basset hound, Milky. No matter which future the reader follows, the same event finds its way in. The project explores how the futures we didn't choose still leave marks on the ones we did.

The site is non-linear. Pages are linked together through hypertext, and readers can move freely, including returning to the starting page by some steps.
